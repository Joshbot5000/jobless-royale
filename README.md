# Jobless Royale

A 3D tower battler where every job is a weapon: 17+ profession arenas, 440+ characters with absurd job-based powers, a deck builder, a Trophy Road, built-in theme music, and a new topical arena every two days based on the news. You play against an AI, entirely on your device.

**Play:** open https://joshbot5000.github.io/jobless-royale/ on your phone, then use Share → Add to Home Screen. It works offline once loaded, and no data leaves your device.

**Develop:** edit `src/`, run `python3 build.py`, then `node tools/test.js`. See `DESIGN.md` for the rules and `UPDATING.md` for the news-arena process.
