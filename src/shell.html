HOW TO PLAY

  • Every arena has 15+ characters and 4 spells. Pick 6 characters and 2 spells for your deck (one 👑 Champion at most). Each arena remembers its own deck.
  • Every character above is yours from the start. Win battles to earn trophies: 5 brand-new characters per arena unlock on the 🏆 Trophy Road at 100, 300, 600, 1000 and a Legendary at 1600.
  • Once your Champion is on the field, its golden ability button appears above your cards. Tap it to unleash its power (costs Broke Bucks, then recharges).
  • Hold a card to read what it does in battle.
  • Drag a card onto your half of the arena, or tap a card then tap the arena.
  • Cards cost Broke Bucks (the cash bar under your cards). They pay out twice as fast in the last minute.
  • Side tower = 1 crown. King tower = 3 crowns and instant victory.
  • Knock out a side tower to deploy deeper into that lane.
  • Power orbs appear on the bridges. The first troop to touch one gets Fire, Turbo, Giant, Shield or Heal.
  • 💼 Career Ladder: every battle pays a Pay Packet of card copies and 💰 Wages. Collect enough copies to promote a card from Intern up to Chief: each promotion gives it +8% health and damage. A green ⬆ on a card means it's ready. Tap it, then tap PROMOTE.
  • 👥 Play a Friend: one of you creates a room and shares the code, the other joins with it. You both pick your deck for that arena before the battle. Friendly matches don't change your trophies.
  • 🏆 Tournament: one device organises and shows a code, everyone joins with it, and the game pairs players at random. Winners go through until one champion is left.
  • 🎓 Career Academy: four special shifts in every arena (take a tower, hold the line, win on a tight budget, beat the Boss). Clear them for big Pay Packets and each job's secret Job File.
  • Tap 💬 to send an emote. Win to earn trophies and stars for each arena.
