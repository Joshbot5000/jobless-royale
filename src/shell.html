HOW TO PLAY

  • Every arena has 15+ characters and 4 spells. Pick 6 characters and 2 spells for your deck (one 👑 Champion at most). Each arena remembers its own deck.
  • Every character above is yours from the start. Win battles to earn trophies: 5 brand-new characters per arena unlock on the 🏆 Trophy Road at 100, 300, 600, 1000 and a Legendary at 1600.
  • Once your Champion is on the field, its golden ability button appears above your cards. Tap it to unleash its power (costs elixir, then recharges).
  • Hold a card to read what it does in battle.
  • Drag a card onto your half of the arena, or tap a card then tap the arena.
  • Cards cost elixir (the pink bar). It refills twice as fast in the last minute.
  • Side tower = 1 crown. King tower = 3 crowns and instant victory.
  • Knock out a side tower to deploy deeper into that lane.
  • Power orbs appear on the bridges. The first troop to touch one gets Fire, Turbo, Giant, Shield or Heal.
  • Tap 💬 to send an emote. Win to earn trophies and stars for each arena.
